Entity–relationship model · Retail & consumer bank, Pakistan

Banking Management System

A complete logical data model for a Pakistani retail bank: branches and staff, customers and KYC, accounts in PKR and foreign currency, debit, credit and member cards, Raast and 1LINK payments, and lending. Every entity has one primary key, exactly ten attributes and ten example values. The entities are joined by fifty foreign keys.
